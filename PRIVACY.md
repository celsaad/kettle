# Privacy Policy

**The canonical policy is [site/privacy.html](site/privacy.html)**, published at
<https://celsaad.github.io/kettle/privacy.html> — that URL is what the Play listing points at.

This file used to carry a second, full copy of the policy. It was collapsed to a pointer on purpose:
two hand-maintained privacy policies drift, and the failure mode is a store listing whose stated
practices disagree with the repo's — which is a compliance problem, not a tidiness one. One source
of truth, in the file that is actually served.

## The short version

Kettle collects nothing, transmits nothing, and has no account and no server. Your library, session
log, preferences and supporter state live in the app's private storage on your device and are
removed when you uninstall it. Tips go through Google Play Billing, so payment details never reach
app code. The website loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics either.

See the canonical policy for the permission-by-permission breakdown and the import/export details.
