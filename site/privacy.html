Privacy policy

The short version: Kettle has no server and no account, collects no personal data, and sends nothing anywhere. This page is the long version.

Last updated 30 July 2026 · applies to the Kettle Android app and this website.

Kettle is a workout tracker built to be backend-free. It has no accounts, no cloud storage and no synchronisation service. Because of that, most of what a privacy policy usually has to explain simply does not apply here. This document states plainly what the app does and does not do with your information.

What Kettle stores

Everything Kettle knows about you is kept in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your library — the YAML file describing your exercises, workouts and programs. You create and edit this; the app reads and writes it in local storage on your phone.
  • Your session log — a local record of completed sets: the date, the exercise, and what you did. You can correct a set you mis-logged, and delete a session outright. Streaks and weekly totals are computed from this log on the device.
  • Your preferences — units, language, theme and similar settings.
  • Supporter state — if you leave a tip, a local count of how many tips you have left, with the tier and date. It records no payment method and no transaction identifier.

None of this is transmitted off the device by the app. There is no copy on a server, because there is no server. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

What Kettle does not collect

Kettle does not collect personal data. Specifically, it does not gather your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, device advertising identifier, or any other personal identifier. It does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to sign in.

Analytics and tracking

There is none. Kettle contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, no advertising library and no third-party trackers of any kind. The app does not build a usage profile and has nothing to send if it wanted to.

Network access

Under normal use the app makes no network requests. It has no backend to contact, and its features work fully offline. This website behaves the same way: it loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or beacons, and it embeds no analytics.

The only time data leaves your device is when you choose to move it — for example, exporting your library or history to a file and sharing it, or copying text out to paste elsewhere. Those actions are initiated by you, go where you send them, and are outside Kettle's control once they leave the app.

Data you import

You can import a library file — one you wrote, or one generated for you by an AI assistant you chose to use. Kettle validates that file against its schema on your device and merges it into your library by identifier. The app itself never contacts an assistant or any external model; any generation you do happens in a separate tool of your choosing, under that tool's own privacy terms, not Kettle's.

Tips and purchases

Kettle has an optional tip jar, and nothing in the app is gated behind it. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing: payment details are processed by Google and are never visible to Kettle. The app learns only that a purchase succeeded, and writes the local supporter state described above. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

There is no receipt verification, because there is no backend to verify against — another consequence of the app having no server rather than an oversight.

Permissions

Kettle requests only what its features need, and each one is used on the device:

  • Notifications — local reminders and a background fallback for workout timers. Scheduled and delivered on your device; nothing is sent to a push server.
  • Vibration — haptic feedback during a session.
  • Run at startup and keep awake — so a scheduled rest timer survives a reboot, and the screen stays on while a workout is running.
  • Storage (Android 12 and older only) — reading and writing library files you import or export.
  • Internet and network state — required by the app framework and used by Google Play Billing. Kettle makes no network requests of its own.

Kettle does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, or health-platform access.

Your control over your data

Because everything lives locally, you are in full control of it:

  • Export your entire library or session history as plain files at any time.
  • Edit or delete any part of your library by hand — it is a text file you own.
  • Remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it, which deletes the on-device library and log. There is no remote copy left behind.

Children's privacy

Kettle does not collect data from anyone, including children, and requires no personal information to use.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page and its “last updated” date will change with them, and the revision history is public in the project's repository. Kettle's design goal — collecting nothing and transmitting nothing — is not expected to change.

Contact

Questions or concerns can be raised in the public issue tracker at github.com/celsaad/kettle/issues. The source code is available under the MIT license, so you are welcome to verify these claims for yourself.
